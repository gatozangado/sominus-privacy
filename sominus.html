Sominus - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Sominus is a Wear OS app that helps prevent drowsy driving and keeps media paused while you sleep, by monitoring motion using your watch built-in sensors.

Data collection

Sominus does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party. All motion and sensor data is processed entirely on your watch, in real time, and is never saved or sent anywhere.

Bluetooth

Sominus uses Bluetooth to send a play or pause signal to a paired device, such as a TV or phone, so that media playback pauses automatically. This works entirely locally between your watch and the paired device. No Bluetooth data is sent to us.

Purchases

Sominus offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock the full version, handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment information; that is handled directly by Google.

Third parties

Sominus does not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party tracking services.
