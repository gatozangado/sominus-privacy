Sominus - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Sominus helps prevent drowsy driving and keeps your media paused while you sleep. It comes as a Wear OS watch app and an Android phone app, plus an optional free Windows helper (Sominus Desktop) that lets the phone app pause a computer. This policy covers all of them.

Data collection

Sominus does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party. We run no servers and require no account. All sensor readings are processed on your own devices, in real time, and are never saved or sent anywhere outside them.

Heart rate and sensors

The watch app reads your heart rate using the watch's built-in sensor. The phone app can receive a live heart rate from your Sominus watch, or from a standard Bluetooth heart-rate sensor (such as a chest strap or a sport watch in broadcast mode). In every case the reading is used only in the moment, on the device, to detect drowsiness. It is never stored, logged, or transmitted to us.

Watch-to-phone streaming

If you enable Phone Stream on the watch, your live heart rate is sent from your watch to your own paired phone using the Wear OS Data Layer, directly between your two devices. It does not travel through our servers - we have none.

Bluetooth

Sominus uses Bluetooth to send a play or pause signal to a device you have paired, such as a TV, and to find and connect to your heart-rate sensor. This works entirely locally between your devices. No Bluetooth data is sent to us.

Local network and Sominus Desktop

If you use the PC pause feature, the phone app sends a short pause or resume command to the Sominus Desktop helper on your own Wi-Fi network. The command contains no personal data. The helper listens only on your local network, needs no internet connection or account, and collects nothing; its small activity log stays on your computer.

Purchases

Sominus offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock the full version, handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment information; that is handled directly by Google.

Third parties

Sominus does not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party tracking services.

Permissions we ask for

Body sensors (watch heart rate), Bluetooth (heart-rate sensors and TV pausing), and network access (finding your PC on your own Wi-Fi). Each is used only for the feature it names.

Changes to this policy

If Sominus gains features that change how data is handled, this page will be updated before those features ship, and the date above will change.
