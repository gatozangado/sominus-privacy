DOWNLOAD FOR WINDOWS
SominusDesktop.exe · 17 MB · Windows 10 and 11
  1. Download and double-click the file.
  2. A message confirms it is running. That is all the setup there is.
  3. In Sominus on your phone, open Media Mode and set OUTPUT: PC (WI-FI). Your phone should find this computer by itself when both are on the same Wi‑Fi.
Windows may show a blue warning

The first time you open it, Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. This can appear for new apps that Windows doesn’t recognise yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Only do this for the file downloaded from this page.

What it does, and does not do

It waits quietly for a message from your phone on your own Wi‑Fi, then pauses what’s playing. No account, no internet connection, nothing is collected or sent anywhere. A small S appears near your clock — right‑click it to quit at any time.

Sominus is a wellness and convenience tool, not a medical device or a safety device.
