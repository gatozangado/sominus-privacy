Pause your computer
when you fall asleep

This small free helper lets the Sominus app on your phone pause whatever is playing on this computer — a film, a series, music — the moment it notices you have drifted off.

DOWNLOAD FOR WINDOWS
SominusDesktop.exe · 17 MB · Windows 10 and 11
  1. Download and double-click the file.
  2. A message confirms it is running. That is all the setup there is.
  3. In Sominus on your phone, open Media Mode and set OUTPUT: PC (WI-FI). Your phone finds this computer by itself.
Windows may show a blue warning

The first time you open it, Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. That appears for any new app from a small developer. Click More info, then Run anyway.

What it does, and does not do

It waits quietly for a message from your phone on your own Wi‑Fi, then presses pause. No account, no internet connection, nothing is collected or sent anywhere. A small S appears near your clock — right‑click it to quit at any time.

Sominus is a wellness and convenience tool, not a medical device.
