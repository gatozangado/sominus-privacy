Sominus Connector - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Sominus Connector is a small utility app that helps a phone recognise a previously paired Sominus watch.

Who we are

Sominus Connector is made by the developer of Sominus. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean that developer; see the Sominus privacy policy for details. You can contact us at sominustech@gmail.com.

Data collection

Sominus Connector does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage data, or device data. The app only reads the list of Bluetooth devices already paired with your phone, entirely on your device. Nothing is sent off the device, to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

The app requests the Bluetooth permission (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT) solely to read your already-paired devices and to open your phone's Bluetooth settings. No other permissions are used.

Third parties

Sominus Connector does not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party services.

If you email us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to sominustech@gmail.com.
