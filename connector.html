Sominus Connector - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Sominus Connector is a small utility app that helps a phone recognise a previously paired Sominus watch as a Bluetooth keyboard.

Data collection

Sominus Connector does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage data, or device data. The app only reads the list of Bluetooth devices already paired with your phone, entirely on your device, to refresh the phone knowledge of your watch services. Nothing is sent off the device, to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

The app requests the Bluetooth permission (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT) solely to read your already-paired devices and to open your phone Bluetooth settings. No other permissions are used.

Third parties

Sominus Connector does not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party services.
